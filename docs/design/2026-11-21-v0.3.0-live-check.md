## Findings

| ID | Finding | Settled |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Every tool call, from the chat as well as from Cowork, was logged with `session` `forward-1`; the owner's own stdio session received none. Desktop appears to route its chats through the shared-pool copy as well. Not a memctl defect: the labels are proven by tests (`stdio` and `forward-<n>`), and both surfaces work. | Observation; no change. |
| S2 | At quit, the owner logged `WARN session ended with an error` (`class` `transport`) for the forwarded session. Cause: `mcpserver.ServeConn` passed the connection to the SDK transport as both reader and writer, so it was closed twice, and the second close (`use of closed network connection`) turned every normal end of a forwarded session into an error. Reproduced on the development VM with SIGTERM and SIGKILL to a forwarder. | Fixed test-first: `f8b3adb` (tests: `TestServer_SPEC0009_R13_forwardedSessionEndsCleanly`, and `TestE2E_SPEC0009_R10_forwarderStdinEOF` now checks the owner log), `f4daf39` (fix: the writer side no longer closes the connection). To be checked again at the next quit. |
